# Background load before each window

Descriptive, not part of the outcome. Generated by `scripts/background.py` from `part_a.windows[*].conditions_before` of the 10 raw runs in [`evidence.json`](evidence.json) (120 windows). `bench_concurrency.py` samples once before each window, after the 2.0 s idle and before the 0.5 s lead, so nothing is sampled during a window.

- **Load averages** are the machine's 1, 5 and 15 minute figures at that moment. They include this run's own threads from the windows before.
- **Processes** are the five with the highest `ps -Ao %cpu=`, named by the last path component of `comm`. `ps` `%cpu` is a decaying average over up to a minute of earlier time, with 100 as one core, so a value says a process was recently busy, not that it ran during the window. A process outside the five is not recorded, and `python` and `claude` are process names as recorded; the data do not say which instance.
- **Windows** are grouped by type. A hetero window is slow if its short P99 is at least 13 ms (as in `tables.md`); the other window types have no state.
